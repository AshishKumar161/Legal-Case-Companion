# Legal Case Companion (LCC) - Account Closure & Data Portability Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Framework:** DPDPA 2023 (Section 12 Right to Erasure)

---

## 1. Voluntary Account Closure
Any verified client or advocate may request complete account closure at any time through the Settings interface or by lodging a priority support ticket under category `ACCOUNT_PRIVACY`.

---

## 2. Pre-Closure Requirements & Data Export
Prior to account termination:
1. **Pending Consultations:** All scheduled consultations must either be completed or cancelled with applicable refunds issued.
2. **Outstanding Payouts:** Advocate accounts must reconcile all pending payouts.
3. **Data Export Package:** Users are provided an encrypted ZIP archive containing all case metadata, deadlines, and uploaded evidence files (Format: JSON + original PDFs).

---

## 3. Grace Period & Permanent Erasure Timeline
* **Day 0 - Account Suspension:** Immediate revocation of session tokens and platform access. Account status changes to `SUSPENDED_PENDING_DELETION`.
* **Days 1 to 30 - Recovery Grace Period:** Litigant may reactivate account if cancellation was inadvertent.
* **Day 31 - Permanent Purge:** Irreversible cryptographic destruction of user credentials, personal identifiers, and evidence files.
* **Statutory Exception:** In accordance with Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST rules, financial transaction records and invoice copies must be retained in an isolated, encrypted compliance archive for 8 financial years.
