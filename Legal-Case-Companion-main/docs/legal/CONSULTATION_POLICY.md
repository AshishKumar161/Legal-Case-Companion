# Legal Case Companion (LCC) - Consultation Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Applicable Service:** Digital Video & Audio Consultations

---

## 1. Scope & Objective
This Consultation Policy sets forth the operating standards, professional etiquette, and technological requirements for digital legal consultations conducted via LCC.

---

## 2. Session Guidelines
* **Duration:** Standard consultation sessions are 45 minutes in length.
* **Punctuality:** Both client and advocate are expected to join the virtual consultation room at the scheduled start time. A grace period of 10 minutes is allowed before an unexcused absence is categorized as a `NO_SHOW`.
* **Environment:** Consultations must be conducted in private, confidential environments to protect legal privilege.

---

## 3. Privilege & Confidentiality
* Video and audio streams are transmitted using end-to-end encrypted WebRTC peer connections.
* **Session Recording:** Consultation recording is disabled by default. If either party wishes to record the session, dual affirmative consent is mandatory via the in-app consent modal. Unilateral recording is strictly prohibited.
* Shared evidence files reviewed during consultations remain protected under legal professional privilege.

---

## 4. Professional Independence & Non-Solicitation
* Advocates participate as independent legal practitioners and retain full professional discretion regarding legal advice offered.
* The platform does not guarantee any specific legal outcome or judicial disposition.
* Advocates shall not solicit clients in violation of Bar Council of India professional standards.
