# Phase XX: Trust, Transparency & Public Regulatory Disclosures

## Overview
LCC exposes a public, unauthenticated Trust and Transparency Center (`/api/v1/trust/disclosures`) to clearly establish legal boundaries, professional responsibility, and technical evidence standards.

## Service Categorization & Boundary Disclosures
1. **Legal Information**: Public statutes (BNS, BNSS, BSA, IPC, CrPC), court procedural rules, and judgment digests provided for general educational awareness. Non-binding.
2. **Legal Assistance**: Workflow tools for organizing evidence exhibits, tracking hearing dates, and drafting case chronologies.
3. **AI-Generated Assistance**: Grounded research assistance. Strictly categorized as **Machine-Assisted Research**. Does not constitute legal advice.
4. **Legal Professional Services**: Legal representation, legal opinions, and court pleadings. Provided exclusively and independently by State Bar Council verified advocates. Platform operates strictly as a technology intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## Deterministic Discovery Ranking Transparency
In compliance with Bar Council of India Rule 36:
- Zero paid placement, featured listings, or sponsored visibility.
- Zero fabricated win rates, success ratios, or unverified star reviews.
- Explainable ranking signals: Verified Bar status (Prerequisite), real-time availability (Primary), practice area domain match (Secondary), verified tenure (Tie-breaker).
