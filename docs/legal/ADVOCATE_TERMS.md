# Legal Case Companion (LCC) - Advocate Terms & Onboarding Agreement

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Applicable Users:** Enrolled Advocates & Legal Chambers  
**Governing Authority:** Bar Council of India & Respective State Bar Councils

---

## 1. Eligibility & Verification
To maintain an active profile on LCC, an advocate must:
1. Be a citizen of India and enrolled with an appropriate State Bar Council under the Advocates Act, 1961.
2. Complete the mandatory Bar Council digital credential verification (Bar Council Enrollment Certificate, Photo ID, Certificate of Practice where applicable).
3. Notify LCC immediately of any suspension, disciplinary proceedings, or change in practice eligibility.

---

## 2. Commission Structure & Payout Terms
* **Commercial Model:** Server-authoritative revenue split under the Consultation Commission strategy.
* **Platform Fee:** LCC retains 15% to 20% of the gross consultation fee as a technology facilitation charge.
* **Advocate Share:** 80% to 85% of the consultation fee is remitted to the advocate's registered bank account.
* **Payout Schedule:** Completed consultations are eligible for payout on a **T+2 business day schedule** following session completion without dispute.
* **Statutory Deductions:** All fees collected are subject to 18% GST (SAC 9982). Advocates are responsible for their own direct income tax reporting and professional tax liabilities.

---

## 3. Professional Conduct & Quality Standards
* Advocates agree to maintain prompt response times for consultation requests (benchmark: < 4 hours).
* Cancellation of scheduled consultations without at least 12 hours notice or emergency court cause may lead to operational review and temporary roster suspension.
* Fake reviews, synthetic ratings, and opaque algorithmic manipulation are strictly prohibited and technically blocked.
