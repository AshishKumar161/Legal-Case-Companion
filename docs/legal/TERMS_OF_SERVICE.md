## 1. Nature of the Platform & Intermediary Status
Legal Case Companion ("LCC") is an electronic platform and technology intermediary operated to assist litigants in managing their legal matters and connecting with verified advocates for independent consultations.

**IMPORTANT LEGAL DISCLAIMER:**
1. LCC is **NOT** a law firm, does not practice law, and does not provide legal advice, legal opinions, or court representation.
2. The availability of advocate profiles on LCC does not constitute legal solicitation, advertisement, or endorsement prohibited under Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules.
3. Engaging an advocate through LCC creates an independent professional advocate-client relationship directly between the litigant and the chosen advocate. LCC is not a party to that engagement.
