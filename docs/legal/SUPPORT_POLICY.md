# Legal Case Companion (LCC) - Customer Support & SLA Policy

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Operational Scope:** Litigant Inquiries, Advocate Onboarding, Billing & Dispute Resolution

---

## 1. Operating Model & Zero-Trust Boundary
Customer support on LCC operates under strict zero-trust parameters:
* Support operators handle inquiries regarding platform navigation, payment failures, consultation scheduling, and court tracking synchronization.
* **PRIVACY BOUNDARY:** Support operators possess **zero database access** to confidential litigation records, client case notes, or uploaded evidence files.
* **Internal Note Isolation:** Internal technical annotations created by operators are strictly compartmentalized and inaccessible to litigants or counterparties.

---

## 2. Priority Levels & SLA Turnaround Targets

| Severity Level | Definition & Examples | First Response Target | Target Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P0 - Critical** | Payment double-charge, security anomaly, platform outage during scheduled consultation | **< 30 minutes** | **< 2 hours** |
| **P1 - High** | Consultation connection failure, advocate no-show, document upload failure | **< 1 hour** | **< 4 hours** |
| **P2 - Medium** | Court sync delay, CNR formatting issue, invoice generation query | **< 4 hours** | **< 12 hours** |
| **P3 - Low** | General feedback, practice area expansion request, usability question | **< 12 hours** | **< 24 hours** |

---

## 3. Communication Channels
* **In-App Support Center (`/support`):** Real-time interactive ticket messaging with chronological operator communication history.
* **Escalations:** Tickets unresolved within SLA boundaries automatically escalate to the Lead Support Operator and Compliance Manager.
